PROBLEM STATEMENT
01 · Lack of Scalable Hands-On Training
Universities struggle to provide scalable hands-on cybersecurity training. Traditional lab environments rely heavily on manual setup and instructor involvement, making it difficult to deliver consistent Red Team experience to large numbers of students.
02 · Lack of Automated & Integrated Platforms
Existing cyber ranges lack automated adversary emulation. Commercial solutions are costly and not designed for education, creating a gap for a cost-effective platform that supports automated attack orchestration and MITRE ATT&CK alignment.
03 · Manual Environment Setup Overhead
Students and lecturers spend significant time configuring environments, installing tools, and troubleshooting issues, reducing time available for actual hands-on learning.

Project Scope
Level Scope Description
BASIC Core Automation Prototype
The platform shall be able to:
1. Deploy a predefined vulnerable lab environment using virtualization or containerization.
2. Execute at least five basic attack modules (e.g., reconnaissance and scanning).
3. Perform manual environment reset to restore initial configuration.
INTERMEDIATE Modular attackwork flow automation
The platform shall be able to:
1. Automate a structured attack workflow.
2. Perform automated environment reset and ensure reproducibility.
3. Integrate with the underlying SOC infrastructure.
3. Collect, store, and display raw attack results.
ADVANCED Full red team orchestration platform
The platform shall be able to:
1. Dynamically select attack workflows based on target configuration.
2. Support concurrent execution on multiple targets or environments.
3. Map attack activities to MITRE ATT&CK techniques and generate structured reports.
3. Integrate logging, performance benchmarking, and automated evaluation metrics.
