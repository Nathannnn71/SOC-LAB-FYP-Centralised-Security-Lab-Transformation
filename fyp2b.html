PROBLEM STATEMENT
Despite the growing demand for cybersecurity professionals, universities struggle to provide scalable hands-on training environments. Traditional lab-based approaches rely heavily on manual setup and instructor involvement, making it difficult to deliver consistent and practical Red Team experience to large numbers of students. This limits the ability of universities to train large cohorts of students effectively. Besides, current lab-based environments require a significant amount of manual set-up and teacher intervention, leading to scalability problems and inconsistency of student learning. Even if there are such lab environment, it may be missing the ability to automate attack scenario execution through an integrated solution. Commercial solutions are typically very expensive and are not geared toward education, creating the need for a low cost platform capable of automating attack orchestration and execution. Thus, there is a need for an platform capable of reducing startup time, improving scalability and providing consistent, repeatable, hands-on cybersecurity practical activities that aligns with industry frameworks, such as the MITRE ATT&CK framework.

Project Scope
Level Scope Description
BASIC Core Automation Prototype
The platform shall be able to:
1. Deploy a predefined vulnerable lab environment using virtualization or containerization.
2. Execute at least five basic attack modules (e.g., reconnaissance and scanning).
3. Perform manual environment reset to restore initial configuration.
INTERMEDIATE Modular attackwork flow automation
The platform shall be able to:
1. Automate a structured attack workflow.
2. Perform automated environment reset and ensure reproducibility.
3. Integrate with the underlying SOC infrastructure.
3. Collect, store, and display raw attack results.
ADVANCED Full red team orchestration platform
The platform shall be able to:
1. Dynamically select attack workflows based on target configuration.
2. Support concurrent execution on multiple targets or environments.
3. Map attack activities to MITRE ATT&CK techniques and generate structured reports.
3. Integrate logging, performance benchmarking, and automated evaluation metrics.
