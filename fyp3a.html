PROBLEM STATEMENT
Cybersecurity students lack hands-on understanding of honeypot concepts, practical experience configuring honeypot systems, and struggle to analyze attack patterns due to complex security dashboards designed for expert analysts rather than learners. This negatively affects students' readiness for SOC analyst roles, as they graduate without practical proficiency in threat detection and attack analysis. The absence of an educational honeypot infrastructure with an attack pattern explanation interface prevents students from bridging theoretical knowledge with practical deployment experience and guided analysis of attack patterns tailored for learning.

PROJECT CONTRIBUTION
This project addresses a critical gap in cybersecurity education by developing an Attack Pattern Explanation Interface that transforms complex honeypot attack data into effective learning experiences. Unlike existing honeypot deployments that use expert-oriented dashboards, this educational system provides simplified visualizations, plain-language explanations, guided analysis workflows, and interactive exercises specifically designed for student learning. The system enables students to develop practical SOC analyst skills through hands-on experience with real attack data, bridging the theory-practice gap in cybersecurity education.
